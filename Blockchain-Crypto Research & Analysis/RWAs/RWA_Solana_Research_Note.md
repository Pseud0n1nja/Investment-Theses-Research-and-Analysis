## 1. Real World Assets: Sector Overview

### 1.1 Market Definition and Significance

Real World Assets (RWAs) represent the tokenization of traditional financial instruments and physical assets on blockchain networks. This emerging sector bridges traditional finance (TradFi) with decentralized finance (DeFi), enabling 24/7 trading, programmable compliance, and enhanced liquidity for previously illiquid assets.
