## 2. PENDLE'S SOLUTION ARCHITECTURE

### 2.1 Yield Tokenization Innovation
**Core Technology:** Pendle splits yield-bearing tokens into two components:
- **PT (Principal Tokens):** Represent the underlying asset, redeemable at maturity
- **YT (Yield Tokens):** Represent all future yield until expiration

**Key Benefits:**
1. **Fixed Yield Creation:** Users can sell YT to lock in guaranteed returns
2. **Yield Amplification:** Buy additional YT to increase yield exposure
3. **Flexible Strategies:** Mix PT/YT positions for customized risk/return profiles
4. **Liquidity Provision:** Earn fees while maintaining yield exposure

### 2.2 Technical Implementation
**Standardized Yield (SY) Wrapper:**
- Normalizes different yield-bearing tokens (stETH, aUSDC, etc.)
- Enables seamless integration with Pendle AMM
- Supports multiple yield sources and protocols

**Advanced AMM Design:**
- Custom AMM optimized for yield token trading
- Time-decay aware pricing for expiring assets
- Efficient capital utilization for LP providers
