# INSTITUTIONAL RISK MANAGEMENT FRAMEWORK
## Liquid Token Investments (<$1B FDV)

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This framework provides institutional investors with comprehensive risk management protocols for liquid token investments in assets with sub-$1B fully diluted valuations. The framework emphasizes position sizing, diversification, and systematic risk monitoring to optimize risk-adjusted returns while maintaining capital preservation principles.

---

## 1. POSITION SIZING FRAMEWORK

### 1.1 Kelly Criterion Application
**Formula:** f* = (bp - q) / b
- f* = fraction of capital to wager
- b = odds (expected return ratio)
- p = probability of winning
- q = probability of losing (1-p)

**Example for STRK:**
- Expected return: 153.5% over 24 months
- Win probability: 80% (bull + base scenarios)
- Loss probability: 20% (bear scenario)
- Kelly percentage: 8.7%
- Recommended allocation: 50% of Kelly = 4.4%

### 1.2 Risk Parity Approach
**Target Allocations by Risk Profile:**
- **Conservative:** 1-3% per position, max 15% total crypto
- **Moderate:** 3-5% per position, max 25% total crypto
- **Aggressive:** 5-8% per position, max 40% total crypto

### 1.3 Correlation-Adjusted Sizing
**Correlation Matrix Considerations:**
- High correlation (>0.7) with existing positions: Reduce by 30%
- Medium correlation (0.4-0.7): Reduce by 15%
- Low correlation (<0.4): Full allocation permitted

## 4. STOP LOSS FRAMEWORK

### 4.1 Technical Stop Losses
**Systematic Rules:**
- Initial stop: -25% from entry price
- Trailing stop: 15% below recent high after +50% gain
- Support level stop: Below key technical support
- Time-based stop: Exit if no progress after 6 months

### 4.2 Fundamental Stop Losses
**Trigger Events:**
- Major smart contract exploit (>10% of TVL)
- Key team member departure
- Significant competitive disadvantage
- Regulatory action against protocol

### 4.3 Portfolio-Level Stops
**Circuit Breakers:**
- Total crypto allocation exceeds target by >20%
- Any single position exceeds 10% of total portfolio
- Portfolio correlation with BTC exceeds 0.9
- Maximum drawdown exceeds -40%

## 5. LIQUIDITY MANAGEMENT

### 5.1 Liquidity Requirements
**Minimum Standards:**
- Daily volume >$1M for positions >$100K
- Market depth >$500K within 5% slippage
- Listed on minimum 3 major exchanges
- Maximum position size: 5% of 30-day average volume

### 5.2 Exit Liquidity Planning
**Tiered Exit Strategy:**
- 25% of position: Can exit within 24 hours
- 50% of position: Can exit within 1 week
- 75% of position: Can exit within 1 month
- Full position: Can exit within 3 months

### 5.3 Market Impact Assessment
**Position Sizing Limits:**
- Trade size <2% of daily volume
- Maximum single day trading: 20% of position
- Market impact target: <1% price movement
- Use TWAP/VWAP execution for large orders

## 6. STRESS TESTING SCENARIOS

### 6.1 Market Stress Scenarios
**Crypto Winter (2022-style):**
- BTC/ETH down -70%
- Alt tokens down -85%
- Volume down -60%
- Duration: 12-18 months

**Regulatory Crackdown:**
- Major jurisdiction bans
- Exchange delisting risks
- Institutional withdrawal
- Duration: 6-12 months

**Technical Crisis:**
- Major protocol hacks
- Infrastructure failures
- Confidence collapse
- Duration: 3-6 months

### 6.2 Portfolio Stress Results
**Expected Losses by Scenario:**
- Market Stress: -65% to -75%
- Regulatory Stress: -45% to -60%
- Technical Stress: -50% to -70%
- Combined Stress: -80% to -90%

### 6.3 Stress Test Actions
**Trigger Points:**
- 10% portfolio loss: Increase monitoring
- 25% portfolio loss: Reduce new positions
- 40% portfolio loss: Consider position reduction
- 60% portfolio loss: Implement crisis protocol

## 7. OPERATIONAL RISK CONTROLS

### 7.1 Custody and Security
**Requirements:**
- Institutional-grade custody solutions
- Multi-signature wallet requirements
- Hardware security modules (HSMs)
- Regular security audits

### 7.2 Counterparty Risk
**Exchange Risk Management:**
- Maximum 20% of portfolio on single exchange
- Tier 1 exchanges only for major positions
- Regular solvency monitoring
- Proof of reserves verification

### 7.3 Operational Procedures
**Trade Execution:**
- Dual approval for trades >$50K
- Real-time position monitoring
- Trade confirmation requirements
- Error trade procedures
