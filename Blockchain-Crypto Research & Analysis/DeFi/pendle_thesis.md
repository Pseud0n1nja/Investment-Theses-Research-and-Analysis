## 2. Sector Context – On‑Chain Fixed‑Income Rails

* **Addressable market:** > $130 B of yield‑bearing crypto assets (ETH staking, stablecoin money‑markets, tokenised Treasuries) vs. ~3 % currently routed through fixed‑yield AMMs.
* **Problem solved:** Splits yield from principal, enabling hedging, leverage and price discovery for crypto and real‑world yields.
* **Competitive moat:** First‑mover network effects (deepest PT/YT liquidity), ve(3,3) gauge wars (Penpie / Equilibria), cross‑chain footprint (Ethereum L1, L2, Avalanche, BSC, Arbitrum, Mantle).

## 3. Protocol Overview

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Launch** | 2021 (tested v1) • Mainnet v2 Jan‑2023 |
| **Mechanics** | Users deposit yield‑bearing tokens (e.g. stETH). Contract issues **Principal Tokens (PT)** and **Yield Tokens (YT)**. Traders swap, lever or hedge future yield. |
| **Fee Model** | 5 % skim on all yield harvested **+ 0.05 % trading fee** • 100 % auto‑converted to PENDLE and distributed to vePENDLE lockers. |
| **Current TVL split** | ~60 % LRT assets (eETH, ezETH, swETH) • 25 % LSD (stETH, rETH) • 15 % others (stablecoin AMM, sDAI, PT‑sBUIDL pilot). |
